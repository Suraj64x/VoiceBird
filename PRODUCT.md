# VoiceBird: product notes

Written from the owner's brief and messages during the build (no separate interview; treat as working assumptions).

- **What it is:** a local, free, unlimited voice-over tool. Paste a script, pick a cloned voice, get a finished narration file. Replaces a paid MiniMax subscription.
- **Who uses it:** one YouTube creator (two channels), not a developer. Wants plain words, few choices, fast results.
- **Where:** a Windows laptop, usually dark room / editing session, next to a video editor. Dark by default.
- **The job, in order:** choose voice → write or paste script (paragraphs split by blank lines) → generate → compare takes per paragraph → export a YouTube-ready file.
- **What matters most:** the voice sounds like the sample and sounds natural; no clicks or volume jumps; one click to export at YouTube loudness.
- **Surface mode:** Operate (a tool, not a marketing page).
- **Tone of copy:** short, plain, no jargon. Buttons name their action.
